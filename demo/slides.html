Destiny Engine

Turn your dream into a map you can walk.

AI life-transformation coach React · Node.js · SQLite · three.js Powered by Claude
Hi, we built Destiny Engine. One line: you tell it where your life is today and where you want it to be, and it turns that dream into a month-by-month map with a coach that walks it with you. The stars in the background are the actual metaphor of the product — every month of your plan is a star. Let's travel through it.

The problem

Everyone has a dream. Almost no one has a plan for this Tuesday.

01

Advice is generic

“Start freelancing” ignores your EMI, your two kids and the 2 hours you actually have.

02

Nobody notices drift

Plans live in a notes app. When week three slips, nothing tells you — so the dream quietly dies.

03

Life breaks plans

One obstacle — no clients, a worried spouse, a surprise bill — and a static plan is useless.

Ask the room: who has a dream they've been "planning" for more than a year? The issue is rarely motivation. Three things fail: advice isn't about YOUR life, nobody notices when you drift, and plans can't bend when life happens. Click through the three cards.

How it works

Four steps from “someday” to week one

  1. 1

    Map your life

    26–29 adaptive questions, one at a time. Skip anything — we estimate it.

  2. 2

    Name your dream

    In your own words, or from 50 dreams people already reached.

  3. 3

    See if it adds up

    Six honest tests: money, skills, family, location, market, time.

  4. 4

    Walk the roadmap

    Phases, months, weekly checklists, daily coaching and re-planning.

Under 10 minutes from sign-up to a full roadmap. The questionnaire adapts — no kids, no school questions; no debt, no credit-score question. Every skip is inferred, so nobody is blocked.

Feasibility

Honest before hopeful

  • Six scored dimensions, each explained with your own numbers
  • Funding gap and months-to-close, from your real surplus
  • Named obstacles and a matched success story
This is the moment users trust it. It doesn't just say yes — it shows the funding gap, the skills gap, and what the family situation means. Even a 60% dream gets a path: the roadmap closes the gaps first.

The roadmap

Your plan is a constellation

  • One star per month, milestones as bright stars
  • 7 actions a week: time, tools, “done when”
  • Income & savings projection, risks and resources
The gold line is how far you've travelled. Click any star to see that month's weeks. Every action has a time estimate, a resource and a success metric — no "work on your brand" vagueness.

Daily coaching

A coach that knows it’s week 6

  • Six message types: nudge, tip, opportunity, motivation, warning, celebration
  • Reads your pace — warns when you slip, celebrates milestones
  • Check off today’s action, close the week, unlock the next
The Today screen is the daily habit loop. The message is generated from live progress — completed actions, pace versus plan, income logged. Closing a week can unlock a milestone with a celebration.

Adaptive planning

When life happens, the plan bends

  • Describe the obstacle in plain words
  • Get a diagnosis, causes, fixes and pivots
  • See the impact first — 7 → 8 months · 93% → 89%, moving day month 6 → 7
  • One click re-plans — completed weeks stay locked, new actions this week
This is our favourite feature. "I sent 25 pitches and got one reply." The coach diagnoses it, proposes four concrete actions, and before you accept it shows exactly what changes: the timeline goes from 7 to 8 months, feasibility is re-scored from 93% to 89% with the reason, and the moving day slides from month 6 to month 7. Completed weeks stay locked; only the future is re-planned. You can keep your original plan instead.

How the AI works

Grounded numbers. Personal words.

Input

Life profile + dream

Money, skills, family, time, location

Planning engine

Deterministic baseline

Runway, scores, timeline, projections — computed, never invented

Claude Opus 5.5

Personalise

Analysis, month plans, coaching, diagnosis — as structured JSON

Guardrails

Validate & merge

Field-by-field checks over the baseline

No key, rate limit or bad output? The engine answers instantly — the demo can’t break.
Important for judges: the LLM never invents your money. Numbers come from a deterministic engine; Claude adds judgement and language on top, and we validate every field before merging. Any failure falls back to the engine, so it even works offline. Streaming, effort levels per task, caching and rate limits are built in.

The stack

Client

  • React 19 + Vite 8
  • Tailwind CSS 4
  • three.js / React Three Fiber
  • TanStack Query, Recharts
  • Lazy routes · 3D only where it’s used

Server

  • Node 24 + Express 5
  • Built-in node:sqlite — zero native deps
  • JWT auth · rate limits · Helmet
  • 25+ REST endpoints
  • Single-port production deploy

AI

  • Claude claude-opus-5-5
  • Streaming + per-task effort
  • Refusal fallback built in
  • Planning engine: 10 archetypes
  • Graceful offline mode
Mention it's a pnpm monorepo: pnpm install, pnpm dev and it runs. SQLite is Node's built-in module, so there are no native builds to fail on someone's laptop.
